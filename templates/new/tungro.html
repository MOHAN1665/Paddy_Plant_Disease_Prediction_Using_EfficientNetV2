Tungro Disease

Caused by Rice tungro bacilliform virus

Disease Information

Scientific Name:
Rice tungro bacilliform virus

Distribution:
Asia, Africa, Americas, Europe, Oceania

Primary Host:
Rice (Oryza sativa)

Other Hosts:
Wild grasses

Symptoms

  • Stunted plants with reduced tillering
  • Yellow or orange-yellow leaves with rust-colored spots
  • Discoloration begins from leaf tip extending downward
  • Delayed flowering with small panicles
  • Most panicles sterile or with partially filled grains
  • Affects all growth stages, especially vegetative
Detection Technique
  • Collect leaf samples at 6 a.m.
  • Immerse top 10 cm in iodine solution (2g iodine + 6g potassium iodide in 100ml water) for 15 minutes
  • Tungro-infected leaves develop dark blue streaks

Pathogen & Vector

  • Transmitted by leafhoppers (Nephotettix virescens most efficient)
  • Associated with rice tungro bacilliform virus (RTBV) and rice tungro spherical virus (RTSV)
  • RTBV particles rod-shaped (100-300nm length, 30-35nm width) with 8.3kb DNA
  • RTSV particles isometric (30nm diameter) with 12kb RNA
Favorable Conditions
  • Presence of virus sources and vectors
  • Susceptible host plants at vulnerable growth stages
  • Synchronization of virus, vector and host presence

Management Strategies

Trap Methods
  • Set up light traps to attract and monitor leafhoppers
  • Spray/dust insecticides near traps in early morning
  • Practice daily during risk periods
Cultural Methods
  • Plant resistant varieties (IR 36, IR 50, ADT 37, Ponmani, etc.)
  • Adjust planting dates to avoid peak vector activity
  • Observe fallow period of at least 1 month
  • Rotate with pulses or oilseeds in epidemic areas
  • Apply neem cake @ 12.5kg/20 cent nursery
  • Destroy weed hosts on bunds
Chemical Methods
  • Spray 2% urea mixed with Mancozeb (2.5g/l) to minimize yellowing
  • Foliar fertilizer like multi-K (1%) for resistance
  • Control vectors with insecticides at 15 and 30 days after transplanting
  • Options: Fenthion 100 EC (40ml/ha), Carbofuran 3G (3.5kg/ha), etc.
  • Treat nursery with Carbofuran granules @ 1kg/ha when infection is low
