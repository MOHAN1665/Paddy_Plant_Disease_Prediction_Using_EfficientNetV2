Brown Spot of Rice

Also known as sesame leaf spot or Helminthosporiose

Disease Information

Scientific Name:
Helminthosporium oryzae

Distribution:
Worldwide in rice-growing regions

Primary Host:
Rice (Oryza sativa)

Other Hosts:
Infected seeds, volunteer rice, weeds

Symptoms

  • Minute brown dots that become oval to circular (resemble sesame seeds)
  • Spots measure 0.5-2.0mm, may coalesce to form large patches
  • Infection on panicle and neck with brown coloration
  • Black/brown spots on glumes covered by olivaceous growth
  • Seed infection causes germination failure and seedling mortality
  • Reduces grain quality and weight
  • Up to 50% yield reduction in severe cases
  • Seedlings may appear brownish and scorched

Management Strategies

Cultural Methods
  • Use disease-free seeds
  • Grow resistant varieties (ADT 44, PY 4, CORH 1, CO 44, etc.)
  • Remove alternate & collateral hosts
  • Amend silicon-deficient soils with calcium silicate slag
Preventive Methods
  • Seed treatment with Pseudomonas fluorescens (10g/kg seed)
  • Seed treatment with Captan or Thiram (2g/kg seed)
  • Hot water seed treatment (53-54°C for 10-12 minutes)
  • Seedling dip in biocontrol solutions
Chemical Control
  • Seed treatment with fungicides (Captan, Thiram, Carbendazim)
  • Spray Mancozeb (2g/l) or Edifenphos (1ml/l) 2-3 times at 10-15 day intervals
  • Spray during early morning or late afternoon
  • Tricyclazole, chitosan, iprodione can be effective
