Unknown Disease Detected

Our system identified potential abnormalities but couldn't confidently classify them

Recommended Actions

  1. Isolate affected plants to prevent potential spread to healthy crops.
  2. Document symptoms with clear photos from multiple angles, including:
    • Whole plant view
    • Close-up of lesions/discoloration
    • Root system (if possible)
  3. Monitor progression daily and note:
    • Speed of symptom spread
    • Environmental conditions (rainfall, temperature)
    • Any insect activity
  4. Consult agricultural experts at:
    Local Extension Office State Agriculture Department

General Prevention Measures

Cultural Practices
  • Use certified disease-free seeds
  • Maintain proper plant spacing
  • Practice crop rotation
Chemical Controls
  • Apply broad-spectrum fungicides
  • Use bactericides if bacterial origin is suspected
  • Follow recommended application schedules
