Blast of Rice

Also known as Rotten neck or Rice fever

Disease Information

Scientific Name:
Pyricularia oryzae

Distribution:
Worldwide in rice-growing regions

Primary Host:
Rice (Oryza sativa)

Secondary Host:
Maize

Symptoms

Leaf Blast
  • White to gray-green lesions with brown borders
  • Spindle-shaped spots (0.5-1.5cm long)
  • Several spots coalesce to form big irregular patches
  • Severe cases give a blasted appearance
Neck Blast
  • Grayish brown lesions on the neck
  • Causes girdling of the neck
  • Panicle falls over (rotten neck)
  • No grain formation if infected before milk stage

Management Strategies

Cultural Methods
  • Plant resistant varieties (CO 47, CO 50, ADT 36-39, ASD 16-20, etc.)
  • Avoid excess nitrogen fertilizer
  • Apply nitrogen in three split doses
  • Remove weed hosts from bunds
Preventive Methods
  • Avoid dry nurseries and late planting
  • Burn straw and stubbles after harvest
  • Seed treatment with Pseudomonas fluorescens
  • Seedling root dip in biocontrol agents
Chemical Control
  • Seed treatment with Captan/Carbendazim/Thiram/Tricyclazole (2g/kg seed)
  • Spray Tricyclazole (1g/l) or Edifenphos (1ml/l) or Carbendazim (1g/l)
  • 3-4 sprays at critical growth stages
  • Systemic fungicides like pyroquilon and tricyclazole are effective
