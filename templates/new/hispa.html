Rice Hispa

Caused by Dicladispa armigera

Pest Information

Scientific Name:
Dicladispa armigera

Distribution:
Asia, Africa, Americas, Europe, Oceania

Primary Host:
Rice (Oryza sativa)

Other Hosts:
Wild grasses near fields

Symptoms & Damage

  • Visible mining by grubs on leaves
  • Scraping of upper leaf surface leaving white streaks
  • Irregular translucent white patches from larval tunneling
  • Withered damaged leaves
  • Severely infested fields appear burnt
Nature of Damage
  • Grubs mine into leaf blades, feeding between veins
  • Adults scrape green matter from tender leaves
  • Primarily affects plants in young growth stages

Pest Identification

Egg
  • Laid inside minute slits on tender leaves
  • Typically found toward leaf tips
Grub
  • Whitish yellow and flattened
  • Feeds inside leaf tissue by mining
  • Pupates inside leaf
Adult
  • Square-shaped, 1/6 to 1/8 inch in size
  • Dark blue or blackish color
  • Covered with spines over entire body

Management Strategies

Cultural Methods
  • Avoid over-fertilizing fields
  • Use closer plant spacing for better tolerance
  • Destroy leaf tips containing blotch mines
  • Manual collection and killing of beetles
  • Cut shoot tips to prevent egg laying
Biological Methods
  • Clip and bury shoots in mud (reduces grubs by 75-92%)
  • Encourage reduviid bugs that prey on adults
  • Maintain natural predator populations
Chemical Control
  • Spray methyl parathion 0.05%
  • Spray quinalphos 0.05%
  • Apply as spot treatments when infestations are localized
  • Use chemicals as last resort to preserve natural enemies
