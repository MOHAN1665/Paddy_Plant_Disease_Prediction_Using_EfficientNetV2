Bacterial Leaf Blight

Caused by Xanthomonas oryzae pv. oryzae

Disease Information

Scientific Name:
Xanthomonas oryzae pv. oryzae

Distribution:
Worldwide in rice-growing regions

Primary Host:
Rice (Oryza sativa)

Other Hosts:
Wild grasses (Leersia species)

Symptoms

Seedling Wilt (Kresek)
  • Appears 1-3 weeks after transplanting
  • Green water-soaked layer along leaf cuts/tips
  • Leaves wilt, roll up, turn grayish-green to yellow
  • Complete plant wilting possible
Leaf Blight
  • Water-soaked to yellowish stripes on leaves
  • Undulated yellowish-white marginal necrosis
  • Milky bacterial ooze visible in morning
  • Up to 60% yield loss possible
Confirmation
  • Squeeze lower end of infected seedling - yellow ooze appears
  • Observe cut portion against light for streaming bacteria
  • Water becomes turbid after 1-2 hours

Pathogen Identification

  • Rod-shaped bacteria (1.2 x 0.3-0.5 µm)
  • Gram negative, non-spore-forming
  • Pale yellow, circular colonies on nutrient agar
Favorable Conditions
  • Weeds, infected stubbles in field
  • Warm temperatures (25-30°C), high humidity
  • Deep water, severe winds causing wounds
  • Over-fertilization, especially nitrogen
  • Spread via irrigation water, rain, tools

Management Strategies

Preventive Methods
  • Seed treatment with bleaching powder (100g/l)
  • Seed soaking in Agrimycin (0.025%) + ceresan (0.05%)
  • Hot water treatment (52-54°C for 30 min)
  • Spray neem oil 3% or NSKE 5%
  • Apply fresh cowdung extract (20g/l water)
Cultural Methods
  • Grow tolerant varieties (IR 20, IR 72, PONMANI, TKM 6)
  • Use disease-free seed from upland nurseries
  • Avoid seedling clipping during transplanting
  • Balanced fertilization (avoid excess nitrogen)
  • Proper drainage (except during flowering)
  • Destroy weeds and collateral hosts
Chemical Methods
  • Bleaching powder @ 5 kg/ha in irrigation water (kresek stage)
  • Streptomycin sulphate + Tetracycline (300g) + Copper oxychloride (1.25kg/ha)
  • Repeat sprays at 15-day intervals if needed
  • Foliar spray with copper fungicides alternated with Streptocyclin (250 ppm)
